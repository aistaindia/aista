# About The Association of Science Teachers

**Current official name:** The Association of Science Teachers  
**Historical continuity:** In Continuation of AISTA (All India Science Teachers' Association)  
**Legacy:** 1956

The Association of Science Teachers is dedicated to strengthening science education, promoting scientific temper, supporting science teachers, encouraging innovation in teaching, and connecting science education with the needs of society.

## Vision
To foster a scientifically aware, innovative and responsible society by empowering science teachers, inspiring learners and promoting meaningful engagement with science and technology.

## Mission
- Strengthen the professional capacity of science teachers.
- Promote scientific temper, curiosity, critical thinking and evidence-based understanding.
- Encourage innovative, activity-based and technology-enabled science education.
- Provide platforms for interaction among teachers, scientists, educationists and students.
- Promote science communication and public understanding of science.
- Encourage environmental responsibility and sustainable development.
- Support student participation in science competitions, exhibitions, projects and related activities.
- Preserve and continue the Association's long tradition of conferences, publications and educational initiatives.
