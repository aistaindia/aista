ABOUT THE ASSOCIATION

A legacy of science education, carried forward by teachers.

The Association of Science Teachers is the organisation’s current registered name. Its history and science-education mission build on the work of All India Science Teachers’ Association (AISTA), established in 1956.

The association originated from the All India Seminar on the Teaching of Science in Secondary Schools held at Taradevi, Himachal Pradesh, in June 1956. The first annual conference was held at Gwalior in December 1956, beginning a long tradition of annual scientific and educational exchange.

Our central focus is capacity building of science teachers and innovation in science teaching, while promoting research, scientific temper, inquiry, humanism and science-based social transformation.

OFFICIAL REGISTRATION

Registration Details

The Association of Science Teachers carries forward the traditions and work of the All India Science Teachers’ Association, established in 1956.

At the All India Science Teachers’ meeting held at Swami Vivekanand Vidyapeeth, Simultala, Jamui, Bihar, on 28 January 2024, a discussion took place on registering the association under a new name due to administrative reasons. The decision was approved again at the meeting held at Buddha P.G. College, Kushinagar, Uttar Pradesh, on 29 December 2024.

At the General Body meeting held in Patna on 7 September 2025, under the presidency of Prof. (Dr.) Rajmani Prasad Sinha, it was resolved to register the organisation as The Association of Science Teachers. The Secretary, Sri Rabindra Prasad Sinha, was authorised to take the necessary steps.

The registration application was submitted to the Inspector General of Registration, Government of Bihar, in October 2025. The Association was registered under the Societies Registration Act, 1860 (Act XXI of 1860), on 30 March 2026.

Rooted in its 1956 foundation, the Association brings together teacher development, science education, research, communication and public awareness through a continuing national platform.

Strengthen the professional capacity of science teachers through orientation, refresher programmes and continuing professional development.

OUR JOURNEY

From AISTA 1956 to The Association of Science Teachers

The proposal to form an Association of Science Teachers from across India originated at the All India Seminar on the Teaching of Science in Secondary Schools held at Taradevi, Himachal Pradesh, in June 1956, under the auspices of the All India Council for Secondary Education.

Contacts made at Taradevi and the work undertaken there led participants to establish a permanent organisation meeting at least once a year in different parts of India. The first annual conference was held at Gwalior in December 1956.

The Association of Science Teachers is the present registered name of the organisation, in continuation of AISTA (All India Science Teachers' Association). The name has changed for administrative purposes; the history, traditions and science-education mission are being carried forward.
