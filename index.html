ABOUT AISTA

A legacy of science education, carried forward by teachers.

AISTA — All India Science Teachers' Association — traces its origins to 1956, when science teachers from across India came together at the All India Seminar on the Teaching of Science in Secondary Schools at Taradevi.

The association was created as a permanent professional platform for science teachers, encouraging collaboration, research, communication and the improvement of science teaching.

Today, AISTA's central purpose is capacity building of science teachers and innovation in science teaching.

OUR PURPOSE

What AISTA stands for

⚗

Capacity Building

Strengthen the knowledge, skills and professional development of science teachers.

✦

Innovation in Teaching

Promote creative, practical and learner-centred approaches to science education.

⌁

Research & Dialogue

Encourage research and provide an authoritative forum for teachers' views on science education.

◉

National Collaboration

Connect teachers, institutions and organisations working for better science education.

OUR JOURNEY

From 1956 to a new digital era

1956

Proposal for a permanent All India Science Teachers' Association emerges at Taradevi seminar.

1956

First annual conference held at Gwalior in December.

1978

Asian Regional Seminar on Integrated Environmental Science held in New Delhi with UNESCO and ICASE.

1988

Asian Symposium on Biology Education organised with ICASE, UNESCO and AABE.

2006

Forty-first conference at Nagpur marks the Golden Jubilee Conference.

2018

Forty-ninth annual conference recorded at B.H.U., Varanasi.

2026+

A new chapter: building a digital platform for India's science-teacher community.
