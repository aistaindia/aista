ABOUT THE ASSOCIATION

A legacy of science education, carried forward by teachers.

The Association of Science Teachers is the present registered name of the organisation, adopted for administrative reasons in continuation of AISTA (All India Science Teachers' Association), established in 1956.

The association originated from the All India Seminar on the Teaching of Science in Secondary Schools held at Taradevi, Himachal Pradesh, in June 1956. The first annual conference was held at Gwalior in December 1956, beginning a long tradition of annual scientific and educational exchange.

Our central focus is capacity building of science teachers and innovation in science teaching, while promoting research, scientific temper, inquiry, humanism and science-based social transformation.

AIMS & OBJECTS

What the Association stands for

⚗

Capacity Building

Help science teachers enhance their competencies through refresher and orientation programmes and professional development.

✦

Innovation in Teaching

Advance and promote effective teaching and learning of science, especially at school level.

⌁

Research & Dialogue

Foster research in science teaching methods, curriculum and science education and provide a common professional forum.

◉

Scientific Temper

Promote scientific method and attitude, inquiry, humanism and awareness against superstition.

View the broader aims and objects from the Association memorandum
  1. Advance and promote the cause of teaching and learning of science, especially at school level.
  2. Foster research in methods of teaching science, science curriculum and related fields.
  3. Disseminate scientific and technological knowledge.
  4. Provide a common medium for persons working in science, technology and social activities to communicate and deliberate for science-based social transformation, scientific temper, humanism, inquiry and reform.
  5. Hold annual conferences, special conferences, workshops, seminars, symposia, study circles, popular lectures, quizzes, fairs and exhibitions.
  6. Institute and hold special memorial lectures.
  7. Institute and give various awards and prizes.
  8. Publish Vigyan Shikshak (The Science Teacher) magazine.
  9. Publish proceedings, journals, transactions, posters, pamphlets, brochures, books and other necessary publications.
  10. Secure and manage funds and endowments for fulfilling the objectives.
  11. Help science teachers enhance competencies through refresher and orientation courses for professional competence and educational excellence.
  12. Spread a positive attitude towards science, scientific method and scientific attitude among people.
  13. Strive for eradication of all forms of superstition.
  14. Promote awareness against processes damaging nature and natural order and promote climate justice for sustainable development.
  15. Motivate students to pursue pure science at university level and create conditions conducive to it.
  16. Work for awareness of disasters and disaster management, and educate people about health, hygiene and pandemics.
  17. Inherit and follow the traditions, ideas and path shown by the All India Science Teachers' Association established in 1956.
  18. Undertake other lawful acts and activities conducive or incidental to the objectives.
  19. Take prior permission from the relevant government departments before opening any institution, as required by rules and regulations.
OUR JOURNEY

From AISTA 1956 to the present Association

1956

Proposal for a permanent All India Science Teachers' Association emerged at the Taradevi seminar on science teaching.

1956

First annual conference held at Gwalior in December.

1978

Asian Regional Seminar on Integrated Environmental Science held in New Delhi in cooperation with UNESCO and ICASE.

1979

Papers presented to an Asian Regional Seminar on low-cost science equipment in the Philippines and an out-of-school science activities seminar in Singapore.

1988

Asian Symposium on Biology Education organised in cooperation with ICASE, UNESCO and AABE.

2006

Forty-first conference at Nagpur marked the Golden Jubilee Conference.

2018

Forty-ninth annual conference recorded at B.H.U., Varanasi.

Present

The Association of Science Teachers continues the legacy of AISTA, with active branches across multiple states and a renewed focus on digital connectivity.

MEMBERSHIP

Become part of the Association.

As described in the supplied rules and regulations, an Indian citizen aged 18 years or above who is interested in the work of the Society and agrees to its rules may apply for membership.

  • Admission fee: Rs. 100
  • Ordinary Annual Membership: Rs. 500
  • Life Membership: Rs. 2,500
  • Application through the prescribed membership form
  • Membership applications are considered by the Managing Committee
